# Lost Cards

## Two cards cut from Gwent

The game files keep full artwork for two Northern Realms cards that never made it into Gwent:

| Card | Art | What is left in the game |
|---|---|---|
| **Ballista Officer** (siege) | [see the art](../img/lost-cards/ballista-officer.png) | The art and a disabled inventory item marked as cut by the developers |
| **Witch Hunters** | [see the art](../img/lost-cards/witch-hunters.png) | The art and a disabled inventory item marked as cut by the developers |

- They have no power, no ability and no text in any language: they were cut before the
  writing stage.
- Their disabled items were meant to be found as loot.
- The Ballista Officer's item reuses the name of the ordinary Ballista, so it would have shown
  up in the game as just "Ballista".

## Art the game never shows

- **A card back:** a dark wooden card back that no screen uses.
- **Inventory icons** ([see them](../img/lost-cards/unused-icons.png)):
  - Decoy, Horn, Scorch and Weather icons for each faction. Every special card in the game is
    neutral, so only the neutral icons are used.
  - Soldier icons for Monsters, Northern Realms and Skellige.
  - A **Scoia'tael siege** icon, although Scoia'tael has no siege unit at all.

The icons look made for cards that never existed: faction special cards and Scoia'tael siege
units.

## Art Better Gwent already uses

The game ships extra pictures for several Tight Bond cards (Blue Stripes Commandos, Crinfrid
Reavers, Poor Fucking Infantry, Catapult, Siege Tower, Impera Brigade, Nausicaa Cavalry) but
shows the same picture on every copy. Better Gwent shows each copy with its own picture, in
matches and in the deck builder.

## Plans

The Lost Cards module is planned in the [Roadmap](../ROADMAP.md): new cards built on what the game
already has (these two cards, the unused icons, the [cut abilities](cut-abilities-and-leaders.md)),
always as our own documented design, added without touching the game's files or saves.
